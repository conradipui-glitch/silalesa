# R6 — путь от подготовки участка до результата

Scope: four existing guides, no new SEO URLs. Buyer sees a short answer, confirmed starting price (or confirmed standard of installation), three actionable steps «что сделать → что прислать → что получите», contextual WhatsApp action and direct related guide/service links. Existing detailed safety guidance remains in optional disclosure, and the acceptance printable checklist retains its URL and complete last-section bullet list.

Facts and restrictions: standard Kvadro delivery within Omsk and placement on blocks are included; no universal gate/crane/foundation specification. Out-of-town delivery, assembly on plot and unusual conditions require individual agreement. Drilling starts at 2,500 RUB per linear metre, not a total estimate or guaranteed depth/flow/quality. No made-up warranty, lead time, testimonials, projects or prices. The no-JavaScript generated HTML must mirror the actionable first screen and print links. Cross-links should be targeted, not generic all-guides navigation.

Acceptance: all four routes, unique three-step scenarios, first-screen price/action, fallback step content, contact URL, canonical/meta/schema/sitemap, no stale address, print checklist and R1–R5 regression. Visual mobile/desktop and actual WhatsApp send are separate manual checks.
